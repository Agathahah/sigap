SIGAP monitoring

How the account-opening fraud model behaved when months 5–7 were replayed as if live: review load against the team's capacity, drift against the training months, and recall and fairness once the fraud labels arrived two months later. Offline replay on the BAF Base dataset for a fictional bank, not live traffic. Model base-20261028-0433-b4e83b, snapshot of 2026-11-03. Code and method: github.com/Agathahah/sigap.

Performance when the labels arrived

Fraud is confirmed about two months after the application, so each month's recall is only known two months later. Release limits: recall at least 0.40 at the 5% FPR threshold, age FPR ratio (applicants aged 50+ vs younger) at least 0.50.
